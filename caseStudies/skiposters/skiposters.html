Mission

The goal was to go beyond the existing concept and create something more engaging, immersive, and meaningful. The vision was to reimagine coding education as an experience that makes learning feel less like a task and more like a game, motivating students to explore, practice, and develop their skills.

Research

The research process focused on exploring gamified learning platforms, such as Duolingo, alongside the structure and design of existing educational platforms. I also researched what motivates students to learn, focusing on how gamification, engagement, and interactive elements can encourage students to stay motivated and make learning more enjoyable.

Design process

Graphic profile

The visual identity combines a clean black-and-white foundation with vibrant neon accent colors to create a modern interface with a subtle fantasy-inspired feel. Magenta, neon yellow, off-white, and black form the core palette, while pink and orange add further contrast. Helvetica Neue was chosen for its clean, modern look and readability, while Cinzel adds an elegant, medieval-inspired touch that reflects the Game of Thrones influence.

Design Approach

A key part of the design approach was to bring the fantasy theme into the gamification experience through illustrations and visual storytelling. The illustrations were hand-drawn and then converted into vector graphics for use in the interface.

The leveling system was designed around a medieval hierarchy, where students progress from a peasant to higher ranks, such as a knight, as they develop their coding skills. Each level features its own illustration, creating a sense of progression and giving students a character to identify with. Both the small chapters map and the big chapter overview was designed as a hand-drawn map, turning coding exercises into a journey through a fantasy world. Inspired by adventure stories, the map creates a sense of exploration and progression, making the learning experience feel like a quest rather than a series of separate tasks.

Interactive prototype

User testing

A brief user test was conducted on the Figma prototype, resulting in a few minor design adjustments. The feedback helped refine the visual hierarchy and improve the user flow, making the interface clearer and easier to navigate. These adjustments helped strengthen the overall user experience.

Final Outcome

The result was a gamified learning platform for coding, designed to make learning more engaging, structured, and rewarding. The dashboard serves as the main hub, bringing together personal statistics, level progression, streaks, and quick access to the platform’s different sections. From here, students can navigate to exercises, notes, and individual chapters while keeping track of their progress. Chapter completion percentages and visual indicators provide a clear overview of how far they have come, helping students stay motivated even when making small steps forward.

The chapter map turns the learning experience into a journey, allowing students to explore exercises and track their progress along the way. In night mode, students can also self-assess their understanding of individual exercises by marking them as difficult, familiar, or mastered. This allows them to reflect on their learning and identify areas they may want to revisit.

Within each chapter, exercises are organised by difficulty and accompanied by educational videos. Students can take notes while learning and use the integrated AI assistant to ask questions or get explanations when something is unclear. They can also bookmark specific exercises to easily return to them later.

By combining visual progression, interactive learning tools, and gamification, Game of Codes aims to make coding education feel less overwhelming and more rewarding. Every completed exercise becomes a visible step forward, encouraging students to keep learning, build confidence, and develop their skills over time.
