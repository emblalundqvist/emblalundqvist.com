Egg Tracker

Egg Tracker is a digital tool designed to simplify flock management for people involved in heritage poultry breeding. Users can register their hens, track egg production, record how long each hen lays eggs, and document events such as illness or death. The app keeps all information organised in one place, making it easier to monitor the flock and compile the statistics required for annual reports, which are traditionally submitted using paper forms.

Mission

The only requirement from my teacher was to design an app related to eggs, leaving the concept and its purpose entirely open to interpretation. As one of my first projects during the second year of my education, it was an opportunity to finally put my design skills into practice after spending the first year primarily focused on coding. The freedom to explore different ideas was exciting, but with so few guidelines, finding a meaningful concept was also a challenge. I wanted to use this creative freedom to identify a real problem and develop a practical solution around it.
