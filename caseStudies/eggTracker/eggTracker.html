Egg Tracker

Egg Tracker is a digital tool designed to simplify flock management for people involved in heritage poultry breeding. Users can register their hens, track egg production, record how long each hen lays eggs, and document events such as illness or death. The app keeps all information organised in one place, making it easier to monitor the flock and compile the statistics required for annual reports, which are traditionally submitted using paper forms.

My Idea

Keeping track of egg production and flock statistics can be a time-consuming task for people involved in heritage poultry breeding. Breeders need to monitor individual hens, record egg production, track how long each hen lays eggs, and document important events such as illness or death. This information is also needed for annual reports, which traditionally require breeders to manually compile data collected throughout the year and submit it using paper forms.

The idea was partly inspired by my dad, who keeps Öland chickens and is involved in preserving the breed. Through him, I became aware of the challenges breeders face when managing and keeping track of all this information. My vision for Egg Tracker was to create a simple, digital tool that would bring all flock-related information together in one place. I wanted to make it easier for breeders to keep track of their hens and egg production throughout the year, while simplifying the process of collecting and preparing the statistics needed for annual reporting.

Design process

Research + Messy sketches

The research process focused on exploring existing tracking apps, such as Lifesum and Apple Health, to understand how personal data and statistics can be presented in a clear and accessible way. I also researched heritage poultry breeding through Kackel.se, where I learned more about the role of gene bank coordinators and how they collect and compile annual reports from breeders. To better understand the process, I studied the existing gene bank report and its structure. This highlighted how time-consuming and complicated the manual reporting process can be, both for breeders and the coordinators who review and compile the reports. I had also seen these challenges firsthand through my parents' experience.

Graphic profile

The visual identity was designed to feel modern, clean, and easy to navigate. Inter was chosen as the primary typeface for its simplicity and readability, creating a clear and consistent typographic style throughout the interface. Helvetica was used specifically for the calendar dates, as its familiar and standardised appearance makes dates feel instantly recognisable and easy to scan. The colour palette combines warm accents of yellow, orange, and red with neutral shades of white, off-white, and dark grey. The yellow also creates a subtle connection to eggs and egg yolks, making it a natural choice for the app's identity, especially against the light mode's white background. The brighter colours add energy and help highlight important elements, while the neutral tones keep the overall interface balanced and easy on the eyes.

Interactive prototype

Final Outcome

The final result was Egg Tracker, a digital tool designed to make flock management simpler and more accessible. By bringing hen registration, egg production tracking, and flock statistics together in one app, breeders can easily keep track of their hens throughout the year. The app also helps simplify the annual reporting process by keeping all the necessary information organised and readily available, reducing the need for manual record-keeping.
